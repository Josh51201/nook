Nook Privacy Policy

Applies to Nook: Home & Life Hub. Last updated October 11, 2026.

Nook is a personal, customizable organizer made by an independent developer. This page explains what information Nook keeps, who can see it, and how you can delete it. If anything is unclear, write to support@mynookapp.app.

What Nook keeps

  • Your email address, which you use to sign in with a one-time code. There are no passwords.
  • Your profile: the name you choose and your picture or icon.
  • What you add to your Nooks: for example wish lists and gifts, notes, Gatherings and Potlucks, pets and their care, recipes, subscriptions, getaway plans, and the settings and look of your Hearth. Some Nooks let you add addresses and birthdays for people in your life, and the Sick Day Nook lets you log temperatures, medicines and doses. You decide what to enter.
  • Photos you choose to add, such as a profile picture or a picture of a card. Nook only reaches your camera or photo library when you ask it to, and only for the photo you pick.
  • Profiles for kids that a grown-up creates and looks after (see Children below).
  • Notices from your Nooks (like reminders), which appear in the app and, if you allow it, as alerts on your phone.

What Nook does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking services.
  • No selling your information, and no sharing it for marketing.
  • No location tracking.

Who can see what

What you keep is private to you unless a Nook shares it with people you've chosen to join. A Burrow (your family or friend group) shares things like Wishbooks and Gatherings with its members, and a Home shares household Nooks with the people who live there. Nook never lets someone see claims on their own gifts, so surprises stay surprises. Nobody outside the people you've invited can see your information.

Services that help run Nook

  • Supabase stores accounts, data and photos, and handles signing in.
  • Resend sends the sign-in code emails.
  • Expo delivers app updates, and may receive technical details such as the app version and device type to send the right update.
  • Apple distributes the app through TestFlight and the App Store.

These services handle your information only to provide Nook and under their own privacy terms.

Children

Nook is not for children to sign up for on their own. A grown-up can make a profile for a child they look after, such as for a wish list or a sick-day log. That information is entered and controlled by the grown-up, and kids don't have their own login unless a grown-up hands the profile over to them, at which point the account follows the same rules as everyone else's. A parent or guardian can delete a child's profile at any time, or write to us to ask.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays until you delete it. To delete your whole account, open Settings in Nook and choose Delete account. It takes effect right away: your profile, Wishbook, Hearth, photos and notices are removed, and any kids you alone look after are removed too. Gifts you had tucked for other people become available again. Backups of removed information may last a short time before they age out.

If you can't open the app, email support@mynookapp.app from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Security

Your information is stored with a reputable hosting provider, sent over encrypted connections, and protected so each person can only reach what they're meant to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what you keep in Nook.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
